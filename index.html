Honest limitation
This free starter uses FFmpeg's delogo filter, not AI inpainting. It estimates/reconstructs pixels around the selected corner region and may leave artifacts, especially over detailed backgrounds. It does not automatically detect a watermark. Use only on videos you own or have permission to edit.
